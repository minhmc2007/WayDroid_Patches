# Licensing

Three layers, three provenances. They are kept apart on purpose so the licences
do not bleed into each other.

| layer | patches | provenance | licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `10-lineage-forks` | 79 | generated from upstream WayDroid commits | GPL-3.0 |
| `15-ours` | 6 | hand-written here | Apache-2.0 |
| `20-upstream` | 53 | copy of upstream `base-patches-35` | GPL-3.0 |

52 of the 53 are applied. The 53rd is
`20-upstream/roms-patches/bliss/frameworks/native/0001`, kept as a conflict hint
because `waydroid-patches.sh` prunes `roms-patches` out of the apply set.

## GPL-3.0

`10-lineage-forks/` and `20-upstream/` are derived from
[WayDroid-ATV/android_vendor_waydroid](https://github.com/WayDroid-ATV/android_vendor_waydroid),
branch `lineage-22.2`, which is GPL-3.0.

`10-lineage-forks/` is generated by `tools/gen-fork-patches.sh` as
`git format-patch merge-base..fork-tip` over the WayDroid forks, so each patch
is that project's upstream commit and inherits its licence.
`20-upstream/` is a copy of upstream's `waydroid-patches/base-patches-35` plus
its `roms-patches` fallbacks and three `hardware/intel/common` patches upstream
authored but never moved into `base-patches-35`. One file is not byte-identical:
`hardware/intel/common/media-driver/0001-Fix-building.patch` is re-emitted with
its `Android.bp` hunk dropped, because no `intel/media-driver` tag ships an
`Android.bp`. Author and date are preserved, so the provenance is unchanged.

GPL-3.0 applies to the patch *text*. The trees they modify are the Android
Open Source Project, licensed Apache-2.0 by Google, and the various upstream
projects each carry their own licence.

## Apache-2.0

`15-ours/` holds the six patches written for this repo, plus
`waydroid-patches.sh` and everything in `tools/`. Full text in
`LICENSE-APACHE-2.0`.

These are not derived from any Waydroid *upstream* commit. Each one fixes an
inconsistency inside upstream's own Android 15 manifest, discovered by building:

- `vendor/gapps/0001` drops `apps` on `prebuilt_apex`, which Android 15's Soong
  does not have
- `external/libsndfile/0001` renames the library module to the `libsndfile12`
  that upstream's own `external/pulseaudio` depends on
- `external/mesa/0001` defines `mesa_gfxstream_aemu`, which the fork's A15+ stub
  omits
- `external/mesa/0002` calls `ld.lld` by absolute path
- `external/mesa/0003` points `LIBVA_DIR` at the libva the manifest actually
  syncs
- `prebuilts/mesa-tools/0001` supplies the `libxml2.so.2` soname that the
  bundled libLLVM wants

`external/mesa/0002` and `prebuilts/mesa-tools/0001` exist on `lineage-23.2` as
`base-patches-36/15-ours`, also Apache-2.0 and also ours, so porting them here
keeps them out of the GPL layers. They are regenerated rather than copied,
because the blob hashes differ.

They are kept in a separate layer from the GPL material precisely so that
Apache-2.0 does not appear to cover upstream's GPL-3.0 work.
